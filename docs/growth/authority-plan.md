## 6. Original Research & Benchmark Data Releases

Nothing builds more enduring organic backlinks and press citations than releasing proprietary, primary technical research.

### Planned Annual / Bi-Annual Research Reports:

1. **The State of Enterprise Video Surveillance in Indian Industry (Annual Report)**
   - *Methodology:* Anonymized operational data and field audit observations from 100+ industrial camera stream assessments across Eastern India.
   - *Key Findings:* Average number of false alarms per night in traditional systems (38 to 65); percentage of cameras with suboptimal mounting angles (42%); bandwidth consumption of attempted cloud uploads.
   - *Distribution:* Released as an ungated research PDF on `/resources/`, accompanied by an interactive executive summary and press distribution to national business dailies (Business Standard, The Economic Times, The Telegraph).
2. **The Indian License Plate Optical Benchmark (ANPR Technical Whitepaper)**
   - *Methodology:* Benchmarking character error rates (CER) of standard OCR models across 5,000 field images of Indian commercial vehicle plates (HSRP vs regional painted fonts vs dirty plates) under varied illuminations (day, dusk, sodium vapor, IR).
   - *Impact:* Referenced by traffic consultants, smart city system integrators, and toll management engineers.
3. **The Edge vs Cloud Video Surveillance TCO Model (Total Cost of Ownership Analysis)**
   - *Methodology:* Rigorous calculation of 3-year enterprise costs for 20, 50, and 100 cameras comparing dedicated high-speed leased line bandwidth + cloud GPU compute against on-premise edge appliances.
