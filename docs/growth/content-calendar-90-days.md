# Resurgenix 90-Day Content & Growth Calendar

This tactical 90-day organic growth calendar is engineered around high-intent commercial capture, technical comparison evaluation, and foundational technical authority. Rather than chasing generic top-of-funnel consumer traffic, every piece directly addresses enterprise decision-makers: Chief Security Officers (CSOs), IT Infrastructure Directors, Plant Heads, and Smart City Mission Program Managers.

---

## Strategic Architecture

- **Month 1 (Days 1–30): High-Intent & Solution Validation**
  *Objective:* Capture buyers actively searching for solutions to acute operational security problems (unauthorized zone intrusion, virtual fencing, ANPR automation, workforce attendance) and establish baseline organic conversion pathways to `/request-demo` and `/request-pilot`.
- **Month 2 (Days 31–60): Industry Verticals & Technical Comparisons**
  *Objective:* Deliver sector-specific architectural context (manufacturing, logistics, smart cities, healthcare) and capture mid-to-bottom funnel buyers comparing deployment models (Edge vs Cloud, Build vs Buy, AI vs Traditional CCTV).
- **Month 3 (Days 61–90): Authority, Governance & Technical Education**
  *Objective:* Cement foundational authority with comprehensive technical pillar content, compliance guides (India DPDP Act), camera hardware compatibility audits, and buyer pilot governance checklists.

## Content Distribution & Cross-Promotion Blueprint

1. **Internal Linking Standard:**
   - Every published article must link to at least **two solution pages** and **one industry vertical**.
   - Every article must feature an inline or terminal CTA driving to either `/request-demo`, `/request-pilot`, or one of the two lead magnets.
2. **Lead Magnet Retargeting:**
   - Visitors downloading the *Pilot Readiness Checklist* or completing the *CCTV Readiness Assessment* receive a contextually targeted follow-up sequence focusing on technical stream feasibility.
3. **Glossary Term Interlinking:**
   - All newly introduced technical concepts (`RTSP`, `ONVIF`, `Inference`, `Edge AI`, `ANPR`, `Virtual Fence`) are systematically hyperlinked to `/glossary`.
