## 2. Explicit Page Inclusions & Justification of Exclusions

### A. Intentional Architecture Choices

1. **Defence & Smart City Canonical Consolidation:**  
   As specified in the brief instructions, Defence and Smart City pages live **exclusively under `/solutions/defence-security` and `/solutions/smart-city-video-intelligence`**. Creating separate `/industries/defence` and `/industries/smart-cities` pages would lead to keyword cannibalization, split backlink equity, and dilute conversion paths. The `/industries` hub displays them prominently and routes directly to these canonical solution pages.
2. **Dedicated `/locations/kolkata-eastern-india` Route:**  
   Resurgenix is founded and headquartered in Kolkata, West Bengal, with operational ties to Kalyani and Eastern India. Having one authentic regional anchor page provides high local search relevance without resorting to deceptive doorway pages.

### B. Rejected Pages & Rationale

1. **REJECTED: 50+ City Doorway Pages (`/ai-cctv-mumbai`, `/ai-cctv-delhi`, etc.):**  
   _Reason:_ Resurgenix has no physical office or deployed personnel in Mumbai or Bangalore at this stage. Automated programmatic doorway pages violate search quality guidelines and damage domain trust.
2. **REJECTED: Brand-Specific Compatibility Pages (`/compatible-with/hikvision`, etc.):**  
   _Reason:_ Compatibility in IP surveillance is standards-based (RTSP, ONVIF Profile S/T/G). Vendor-specific pages would result in 90%+ duplicated boilerplate text. Instead, compatibility is comprehensively detailed in `/how-it-works` and `/resources/how-ai-video-analytics-works`.
3. **REJECTED: Standalone "Pricing" Page (`/pricing`):**  
   _Reason:_ Per brief truth rules, public standard pricing is not displayed. Enterprise pricing depends on camera channel count, site complexity, and on-premise hardware topology. Commercial inquiries are directed to `/request-demo` and `/request-pilot`.
